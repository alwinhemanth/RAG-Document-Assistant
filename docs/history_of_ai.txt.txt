Artificial Intelligence (AI) began as a formal field of study in 1956 at a conference at Dartmouth College. Early pioneers like John McCarthy and Marvin Minsky were optimistic about the future of machine intelligence. During the 1960s and 70s, researchers developed programs that could solve algebraic word problems and prove logical theorems. However, the field faced several "AI Winters" where funding and interest shifted away due to limited computing power. It wasn't until the 2010s, with the rise of Big Data and powerful GPUs, that Deep Learning became the dominant approach in the field.
