The Transformer architecture was introduced in 2017 by researchers at Google in the paper "Attention Is All You Need." Unlike previous models like RNNs that processed text sequentially, Transformers use a mechanism called "Self-Attention" to process all words in a sentence simultaneously. This allows the model to understand the relationship between words regardless of how far apart they are in a text. This breakthrough led to the development of Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT-4, Claude, and Gemini, which have revolutionized Natural Language Processing.
